Privacy Policy – Webcam Mute Hotkey

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Webcam Mute Hotkey is developed by Murtadha Qahtan (Microsoft Store publisher “rul”).

Webcam Mute Hotkey does not collect, record, store, transmit or share any personal data or camera images.

  • Offline only. The app runs locally on your PC. It never connects to the internet for its features. It has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash reporting, accounts or third-party SDKs. (Microsoft Store handles purchase; the app itself does not phone home.)
  • No data sent. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere.
  • Camera permission only. The app only changes the Windows camera-permission setting (the CapabilityAccessManager webcam consent values). It does not access, view, record, or transmit the camera feed. On a normal exit, it restores camera access (Allow).
  • Settings (the shortcut and start-with-Windows preference) are stored in a local file on your PC only and are removed when you uninstall.
  • Children. The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Microsoft Store; the developer receives no payment details.
